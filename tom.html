PETER-ONE (EARTH-616) ("THE BRAND NEW DAY ERA!")

Portrayed by Tom Holland, this version of Peter Parker brings a youthful, high-tech, and Avengers-level scale to the web-slinger. Introduced as a high school sophomore from Queens, this Peter is recruited into the big leagues by Tony Stark himself. Armed with cutting-edge Stark nanotechnology, mechanical web-shooters, and advanced AI-assisted suits, he battles threats alongside Earth's Mightiest Heroes across the globe and even in deep space.

Despite his access to billionaire-backed equipment, Tom’s portrayal captures the true essence of Spider-Man: a kid struggling to balance geometry homework with saving the neighborhood. His arc is defined by intense growth through immense tragedy—losing his mentor Iron Man and his beloved Aunt May. Following a devastating multiversal crisis, he makes the ultimate heroic sacrifice, forcing Doctor Strange to cast a spell that erases Peter Parker from the memory of the entire world, leaving him completely alone but more determined than ever.

Fast Facts

  • Universe: Earth-616 (Marvel Cinematic Universe)
  • Signature Trait: High-tech gadget suit integration, nanotech armour, and deep ties to the Avengers.
  • Key Villains: Mysterio, Vulture, and the multiversal Sinister Six, Norman Osborn, scorpion (appears during the end credits of homecoming).
  • Love Interest: Michelle Jones-Watson (MJ).
  • Day Job: Completely anonymous citizen. Following the global mind-wipe, he has no official identity papers, lives in a tiny, run-down apartment, and studies for his GED while sewing his own classic, low-tech comic-accurate suit.
