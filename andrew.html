<> Peter Parker (Earth-616, 96283, 120703)

FILE ID: PETER PARKER (EARTH-120703) aka "THE WEB HEAD"

Portrayed by Andrew Garfield, this version of Peter Parker brings a brilliant, high-energy, and deeply emotional depth to the web-slinger. A teenage science prodigy at Midtown Science High School, this Peter is an isolated outsider haunted by the mysterious disappearance of his parents. His life changes forever when he sneaks into Oscorp Industries and is bitten by a genetically engineered spider, granting him superhuman agility, hyper-fast reflexes, and a highly predictive Spider-Sense.Unlike his multiversal variant Peter-Two, this Spider-Man relies entirely on his own scientific brilliance to build mechanical web-shooters and compressed chemical web-fluid.

Andrew’s portrayal stands out for its fast-talking, sarcastic wit during battle, masking a deeply traumatized hero who carries immense guilt over failing to save his first love, Gwen Stacy. Following the tragic events of his home universe, he makes a deeply redemptive multiversal appearance in Spider-Man: No Way Home, finally finding closure alongside his spiritual brothers.
