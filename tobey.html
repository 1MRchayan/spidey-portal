Tobey Maguire's Spider-Man — often affectionately called the "OG" or "Peter-Two"

Portrayed by Tobey Maguire, this version of Peter Parker redefined the superhero genre in the early 2000s. Bitten by a genetically modified spider on a high school field trip, the nerdy orphan transforms into a powerhouse vigilante driven by his late Uncle Ben’s timeless mantra: "With great power comes great responsibility."

Unlike other cinematic iterations, this Spider-Man famously generates organic webbing straight from his wrists rather than relying on tech gadgets. Maguire’s portrayal resonates deeply because it focuses heavily on Peter's human vulnerabilities—ranging from his unrequited love for Mary Jane Watson to losing his job as a Daily Bugle photographer. After a historic trilogy run, Maguire made a triumphant multiversal return in Spider-Man: No Way Home.

Fast Facts

  • Universe: Earth-96283 (Sam Raimi Trilogy)
  • Signature Trait: Organic web-generation (no mechanical web-shooters required)
  • Key Villains: Green Goblin, Doctor Octopus, Sandman, and Venom
  • Love Interest: Mary Jane Watson
  • Day Job: Freelance photographer for the Daily Bugle
